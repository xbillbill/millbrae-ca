Before reserving

Common questions.

Can I use ordinary daily BART parking for a multi-day SFO trip?

Use BART’s Single/Multi-Day Reserved product for an airport-length stay. Daily Fee parking is for stays up to 24 hours and is not meant for several consecutive travel days.

Does the BART calculator include train fares?

Yes. It multiplies the editable one-way fare by two and by the number of travelers, then adds the charged parking days. The default $5.85 fare was returned by BART’s official fare API on August 16, 2026.

Why does the private-lot result say “base”?

ParkSFO’s listed $20.95 daily rate excludes taxes and fees. Enter those from the live quote—or paste another operator’s final all-in total—before comparing.
