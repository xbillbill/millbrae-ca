A better local reference

Useful does not have to mean overconfident.

Every topic has an owner, a source trail, and a visible freshness cue where information may change. Official information belongs with the institution that maintains it; Millbrae Local’s job is to add orientation, context, and a clean path to the answer.

Have a correction or a story lead? Share the local starting point through the directory; publication still requires a named, checkable source.
