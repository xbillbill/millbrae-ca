How the pieces connect

From land and rail to a connected civic life.

Land and name

City cultural-resources material traces the area from the 1835 Sanchez land grant to the Mills estate and the name “Mill brae,” or “mill’s rolling hills.”

Rail and place

Rail service and the depot helped turn a Peninsula landscape into a connected town. The station’s history still shapes how people understand Millbrae today.

City and records

Incorporation made public decisions, budgets, meetings, and services part of a formal civic record—the kind of material found in City archives and government pages.

Community and culture

Libraries, museums, media, schools, and cultural groups make the history usable: they preserve memory, teach it, record public life, and create new traditions.
