MILLBRAE CIVIC LIFE · PRACTICAL GUIDE

Find your civic doorway.

You do not need to understand every department before asking a question, attending a meeting, reporting a problem, or joining a commission. Start with the type of action, then use the institution that owns the next step.

Checked: August 21, 2026. Meeting dates, vacancies, procedures, and contact details can change. The official City page linked in each section is the authority immediately before you act.

Five common civic questions.

The fastest path is usually the one that matches the decision or service you need.

ATTEND

When is the meeting?

READ

Use the Agenda Center for agendas, packets, staff materials, minutes, and meeting records. An agenda is a notice of business—not proof that a proposal was adopted.

REQUEST

How do I report a non-emergency issue?

Use Millbrae Works for potholes, streetlights, public-space problems, and other service requests. Do not use it for an emergency; call 911 when there is immediate danger.

01 · Before a meeting

Read the record before forming the question.

A useful public comment or conversation usually begins with the same small research loop: find the meeting, open the agenda and packet, identify the staff report or attached document, and note what decision—if any—is scheduled.

Calendar

Find the date, time, location, and meeting category.

Agenda

Identify the numbered item and the body responsible for it.

Packet

Read the staff report, map, draft, contract, or resolution attached to the item.

Outcome

Return for minutes, a resolution, or an adopted notice before saying what happened.

Use the live agenda updates for orientation, then open the official Agenda Center ↗ for the controlling record.
