Before reserving

Common questions.

Can I use ordinary daily BART parking for a multi-day SFO trip?

Use BART’s Single/Multi-Day Reserved product for an airport-length stay. Daily Fee parking is for stays up to 24 hours and is not meant for several consecutive travel days.

Does the BART calculator include train fares?

Yes. It multiplies the editable one-way fare by two and by the number of travelers, then adds the charged parking days. The default $5.85 fare was returned by BART’s official fare API on August 16, 2026.

Why does the private-lot result say “base”?

ParkSFO’s listed $20.95 daily rate excludes taxes and fees. Enter those from the live quote—or paste another operator’s final all-in total—before comparing.

How much is SFO Long-Term Parking for 3 days?

At the current $27 maximum per 24 hours, three parking periods total $81 before any live prebooking promotion. The fourth period would bring the posted-rate total to $108.

How much is one week of SFO Long-Term Parking?

Seven 24-hour parking periods total $189 at the current posted rate. A live prebooked quote can differ, so compare the reservation total rather than assuming the posted-rate math will be cheapest.

How much is two weeks of SFO Long-Term Parking?

Fourteen 24-hour parking periods total $378 at the current posted rate. For a trip that long, compare live official-airport, off-airport, BART, rideshare, and park-and-fly totals.

How do I check a parking coupon or promo code?

Use the SFO parking discount calculator to combine free days, percentage savings, taxes, and fees, then compare the estimate with the final checkout quote.

What if I need a hotel before an early flight?

Use the Park and Fly break-even calculator to compare a hotel package with a room plus separate airport parking.

Is parking cheaper than taking Uber or Lyft?

It depends mainly on trip length and your two live ride quotes. Use the SFO parking vs rideshare calculator to find the break-even parking day.
