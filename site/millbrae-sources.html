EDITORIAL METHOD

How a page earns a place in the hub.

1 · FIND

Start with the primary record.

Use the agency, institution, business, meeting document, calendar, map, or feed that owns the information.

2 · EXPLAIN

Add orientation, not invented certainty.

Translate the source into a useful local path, while distinguishing documented fact, current status, and interpretation.

3 · LABEL

Keep source roles visible.

Live feed, official source, editorial guide, community submission, and paid placement are different labels with different responsibilities.

4 · CORRECT

Update when the evidence changes.

Corrections, source suggestions, and story leads are welcome. We preserve the source trail and revise the page when a checkable source supports the change.

For current civic corrections or source leads, begin with the civic and news desk or the relevant agency source listed above. For business listing changes, use the self-service listing flow.
