MILLBRAE REFERENCE · PLACE GLOSSARY

The words that make Millbrae legible.

Some names are formal planning or facility terms. Others are useful local shorthand. This glossary keeps those roles visible and points to the guide that can answer the deeper question.

Checked: August 21, 2026. Place names, facility details, project status, and boundaries can change or mean different things in different decisions. Follow the linked maintained source when precision matters.

A · The connecting places

Start with movement and geography.

These terms recur across transit, planning, neighborhoods, and everyday orientation.

TRANSIT

Millbrae Station

The regional interchange where BART, Caltrain, SamTrans, and SFO access meet. Use it as a transit term first; the surrounding planning area has its own formal boundaries and documents.

Open the station guide →

CORRIDOR

El Camino Real

A historic route and current commercial corridor. Planning, zoning, business, and transportation questions may refer to different stretches or documents.

Read planning and growth →

AIRPORT EDGE

SFO

San Francisco International Airport borders Millbrae’s eastern context and shapes hotels, highways, transit, jobs, noise, planning, and visitor movement. It is next door, but it is not Millbrae.

Read the station story →

PLANNING

Station Area

Usually shorthand for the area addressed by the Millbrae Station Area Specific Plan. Use the City plan when a question concerns a project, land use, or formal boundary.

Open the City plan ↗

B · The public-space names

Places carry history as well as amenities.

TRAIL

Spur Trail

A multi-phase trail and open-space property with a civic history tied to an earlier road proposal. Current access, facilities, and route details belong with the City facility page.

Read the public-space story →

PARK

Mills Estate Park

Also known as Sebastian Park in City project materials, this hillside open space appears in the park and project story. Project status is not the same as current facility access.

Check City projects ↗

NEIGHBORHOOD EDGE

Bayside Manor

A place anchor on Millbrae’s eastern side, near the airport and station-area context. Use formal planning or facility sources for boundaries, projects, and services.

Read the neighborhoods guide →

CULTURE

Mosta Grove

A public-space name that also points toward Millbrae’s relationship with Mosta, Malta. Parks, sister-city history, and cultural memory overlap here.

Read the global-connections story →

C · The institutions

Some local terms are doors, not destinations.

Millbrae Works

The City’s non-emergency service-request path for issues such as potholes, streetlights, and public-space problems. Keep emergencies with 911.

Open Millbrae Works ↗
Millbrae Library

A public learning institution with current services, programs, digital help, Wi‑Fi, and a makerspace. Use the live library page for hours and events.

Open the library guide →
Millbrae Train Museum

A history institution connected to the 1906 depot fire, the 1907 replacement depot, and the city’s rail story. The history guide explains how to distinguish the museum, depot, and current station.

Read history and culture →
City Clerk

The civic records and participation doorway for agendas, minutes, resolutions, elections, public inquiries, language assistance, and public records pathways.

Read the civic participation guide →

D · How to use a place name

Match the term to the decision.

If you are asking…Use…Because…
How do I get there?Station, park, library, or business addressA practical destination needs current access details.
What is formally planned here?General Plan, specific plan, zoning, or project pageInformal labels are not official boundaries.
Why is this place named that way?History, culture, museum, or City sourceNames can preserve people, decisions, and memory.
What is happening now?Live calendar, alert, news, or agency pageCurrent events and conditions need a fresh source.
