PLACES & PUBLIC LIFE

Read Millbrae through the places people use.

A city is more than its boundaries. Its parks, recreation center, trail, station, schools, downtown, and civic facilities reveal how people move, meet, play, and belong.

Last checked: August 21, 2026. City facilities, hours, programs, and event schedules can change; this guide keeps the official links close so readers can confirm before they go.

The city’s public-space system

Thirteen parks, a recreation network, and a trail-shaped edge.

The City of Millbrae describes its Parks Division as responsible for thirteen parks, civic facilities, the Spur Trail, medians, downtown and commercial landscaping, and thousands of trees. That is a useful reminder that “parks” here means more than a list of playgrounds: it is part of the city’s everyday public infrastructure.

Public recreation around Millbrae also crosses city boundaries and agencies. Junipero Serra Park and the Crystal Springs Regional Trail are San Mateo County Parks destinations near the city, with their own hours, regulations, closures, and reservation systems.

Primary sources: City of Millbrae Parks · San Mateo County Junipero Serra Park · Crystal Springs Regional Trail. Use the responsible agency’s facilities page for current amenities, maps, closures, and conditions.
