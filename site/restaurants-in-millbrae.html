Before you choose

Useful context.

Are these the best restaurants in Millbrae?

This is an unranked starting point, not a claim that six places are objectively “best.” The guide favors variety, clear first-party information, and useful choices for common meal situations.

Which restaurant is closest to Millbrae BART?

Fiddlers Green explicitly describes itself as a short walk from Millbrae BART and Caltrain. Check a live map for your exact route, especially if you have luggage or limited time.
