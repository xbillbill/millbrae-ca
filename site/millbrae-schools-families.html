SCHOOLS & FAMILIES

Find the right school-year answer faster.

School calendars, district programs, recreation, youth sports, meals, language support, and family events live in different places. This guide connects them without pretending one page can replace the institutions that maintain them.

Last checked: August 21, 2026. School-year dates, registration windows, eligibility, and program availability change. Always confirm details with the district or City department before relying on them.

Start with the district

Millbrae’s public-school story is a community system.

The Millbrae Elementary School District’s official site brings together Green Hills, Lomita Park, Meadows, Spring Valley, and Taylor Middle School, along with district information, calendars, family resources, and school news.

The district describes its mission around a strong academic foundation, equity through access and opportunity, and helping students learn and thrive. That is the right starting point for understanding the system; current enrollment, placement, and services should come from the district directly.

Primary source: Millbrae Elementary School District. For district-wide and school-site dates, use the official calendar. The district’s school directory lists the Lomita Park campus mailing address as San Bruno even though MESD serves the Millbrae community; use that maintained directory for exact campus addresses.

A simple research habit

Separate school facts from school-year logistics.

  1. Identify the school or district.MESD covers TK–8; Mills and SMUHSD cover high school. Calendar, enrollment, services, and site contacts may live in different places.
  2. Check the date.School-year information ages quickly; record the academic year and last-checked date.
  3. Confirm with the maintained source.Use Millbrae Local for orientation, then use the district or City for the decision.
