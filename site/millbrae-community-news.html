THE MILLBRAE COMMUNITY NEWS DESK

Find the signal without losing the source.

Millbrae news arrives through City announcements, meeting records, school and library channels, community television, alerts, and independent reporting. Each source has a different job; knowing which one you are reading is part of knowing the story.

Editorial rule: An official announcement tells you what an institution says. A meeting record tells you what was proposed or decided. Independent reporting adds another reporting process and point of view. None should be silently presented as the others.

A simple verification habit

Ask three questions before sharing.

Who owns the information?

Is it a City department, school district, library, nonprofit, newsroom, business, or anonymous account? Ownership helps you understand the source’s role and limits.

What kind of thing is it?

Label it accurately: announcement, allegation, agenda item, adopted decision, event listing, opinion, video, or reported story. These are not interchangeable.

What date and document support it?

Look for a publication date, meeting date, attached staff report, official link, named reporter, or direct statement. If the date is missing, keep the claim provisional.

What would change the answer?

For emergencies, service disruptions, elections, schedules, and active projects, identify the agency or record that can update the information.
