The quick answer

One station, three useful connections.

Millbrae Station is at 200 North Rollins Road. BART connects the station with SFO; Caltrain connects north toward San Francisco and south toward Silicon Valley; SamTrans provides local bus connections. BART and Caltrain share the station, so rail transfers do not require a trip across town.

Flying from SFO

Use BART from Millbrae and follow the live destination signs. At SFO, BART arrives at the International Terminal; AirTrain connects to the other terminals.

Common questions

Before you leave.

Does BART go from Millbrae to SFO?

Yes. Millbrae Station is served by BART trains connecting with SFO. Service patterns and transfer details can vary, so check the official BART trip planner and station departure board for your travel time.

Can I leave my car overnight at Millbrae BART?

Use BART’s single/multi-day reserved product for qualifying overnight trips and follow the current parking terms. Ordinary daily parking does not authorize unrestricted overnight storage.

Where is the SFO BART station?

It is at the International Terminal. SFO’s AirTrain connects the BART area with other airport terminals.

Is Millbrae the transfer point between BART and Caltrain?

Yes. Both systems use Millbrae Station. Check the official transfer timetable because connection windows and service patterns can change.
