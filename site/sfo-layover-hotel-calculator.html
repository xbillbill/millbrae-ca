Before leaving the terminal

Common questions.

Can I stay inside SFO overnight?

SFO says the airport is open 24 hours, but only ticketed passengers are permitted from 6:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Security checkpoints close after the last flight in the boarding area they serve, so verify whether your itinerary lets you remain or return airside.

How much arrival time should I subtract?

Include deplaning plus any immigration, baggage claim, customs, bag recheck, or terminal navigation. A domestic carry-on connection may be faster than an international self-transfer; use the conservative itinerary-specific number.

How early should I return for the next flight?

SFO says to check the airline directly. Most airlines suggest two hours for domestic departures and three hours for international departures; enter the rule that applies to your flight.

Where can I compare nearby hotels?
