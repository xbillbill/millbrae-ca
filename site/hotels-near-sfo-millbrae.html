Parking math

Compare the package total—not the word “free.”

A park-and-fly package may be cheaper than a room plus separate airport parking, but only after you count the full stay. Check these five items before booking:

  1. Total room rate after taxes and fees
  2. Exact number of parking days included
  3. Airport transfer method, credit, and operating limits
  4. Extra-day parking rate if the trip runs longer
  5. Cancellation terms and whether the rate is refundable

Before booking

Common questions.

Which Millbrae hotel is closest to BART?

Residence Inn says its entrance is 300 steps from the station platforms. Fairfield says BART is within a block, and The Dylan describes itself as steps from BART and Caltrain. Check a live map for your exact walking route.

Do these Millbrae hotels have airport shuttles?

Residence Inn and Fairfield explicitly say they do not provide an SFO shuttle. The Dylan and El Rancho advertise transportation credits on qualifying park-and-fly packages. Confirm the current transfer arrangement directly before booking.

Can I leave my car while flying from SFO?

Fairfield, The Dylan, and El Rancho advertise qualifying stay-and-park packages. Ordinary overnight room parking is not automatically long-term airport parking, so book the correct package and confirm the included dates.

Are these affiliate booking links?

No. The links currently go to official hotel websites, and Millbrae Local receives no commission. Any future commercial relationship will be disclosed.

Is an airport hotel worth it for an early SFO flight?

Compare the actual alarm times. The early-flight hotel calculator shows the sleep gained and cost per extra hour using your travel and transfer times.

Is my SFO layover long enough for a hotel?

Use the layover hotel calculator to subtract deplaning, bags, transfers, check-in, airport return, and the next-flight buffer from the scheduled connection.
