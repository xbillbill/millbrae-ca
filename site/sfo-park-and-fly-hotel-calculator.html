Before booking

Five terms can reverse the result.

Included parking clock

Confirm whether the allowance means calendar days, nights, or 24-hour periods.

Extra-day rate

Price every day beyond the package allowance, including a late return.

Transfer mechanics

A reimbursement credit is not the same as a complimentary shuttle.

Cancellation window

Package rates can have stricter cancellation terms than ordinary room rates.

Parking eligibility

Book the named parking package; an ordinary room reservation may not allow extended parking.

All-in quote

Use totals after taxes and mandatory fees on both sides of the comparison.

Calculator help

Common questions.

What is the break-even package quote?

It is the highest package room quote that keeps the package's all-in cost equal to the entered room-only, separate-parking, and transfer plan. A lower package quote favors the package; a higher one favors the separate plan.

Does “free parking” mean the package is cheaper?

No. The parking is bundled into the package price. Compare the final package quote with the room-only quote plus every separate parking and transfer cost.

Why does the calculator start with $27 per day?

SFO currently lists a $27 maximum per 24 hours for Long-Term Parking, including taxes and fees. Replace it with the final rate from any parking option you are actually considering.

Can I compare BART or an off-airport lot instead?

Yes. Use the SFO parking calculator to get the separate parking-and-transfer total, then enter the relevant daily and transfer assumptions here.

What if I am deciding between airport parking and rideshare?

Use the SFO parking vs Uber or Lyft calculator to compare both live ride quotes with parking, gas, tolls, discounts, and tip.
