THE MILLBRAE KNOWLEDGE HUB

Learn the place behind the address.

Millbrae is a small city with a long memory, a working downtown, an unusual rail connection, and a community story that is still being written.

How we work: This page is a starting map, not a final word. We separate sourced facts from interpretation, link to primary records, and add a clear date when information may change.

A short history

From rancho land to rail-connected city.

1835

The land containing present-day Millbrae was granted to José Antonio Sánchez, according to the City’s cultural-resources history.

“Mill brae”

The city’s name is associated with the Mills estate and the phrase “mill’s rolling hills,” a reference to the landscape around Darius Ogden Mills’s property.

1860s–1906

The Southern Pacific railroad arrived in the 1860s; the train depot built in 1906 remains part of the city’s transportation story and is now home to the Millbrae Train Museum.

1948

Millbrae incorporated on January 14, 1948. The date is supported by the City’s current facts page, San Mateo County LAFCO, and a California appellate decision describing the incorporation certificate filed with the Secretary of State.

Primary history source: City of Millbrae cultural-resources document. Date cross-checks: City About Millbrae · San Mateo County LAFCO · California appellate decision. A later City information document lists 1949; the independent county and court records support 1948.

Millbrae at a glance

Direct answers, with the source nearby.

These are orientation answers, not substitutes for the agencies and records that own changing details.

Where is Millbrae?

Millbrae is in San Mateo County on the San Francisco Peninsula, immediately west of San Francisco International Airport. The City describes its location as south of San Francisco and north of Silicon Valley. City source ↗

When was Millbrae incorporated?

January 14, 1948. That date appears on the City’s current facts page, San Mateo County LAFCO’s city profile, and a California appellate decision describing the incorporation certificate filed with the Secretary of State. One later City document says 1949, but the independent county and court records support 1948. City source ↗ · County LAFCO source ↗ · Court decision ↗

Why is Millbrae important for transit?

Millbrae Station connects BART, Caltrain, SamTrans, and SFO access in one regional interchange. Use the station guide for orientation and live estimates, then confirm disruptions with the responsible agency. Station guide →

How does Millbrae city government work?

Millbrae uses a Council–Manager form: the elected Council sets policy and the City Manager administers daily operations. Government guide →
