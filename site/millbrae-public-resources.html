PUBLIC RESOURCES

Find the right public door.

City life is full of small questions: a water issue, a streetlight, a library card, a transit connection, a permit, a public meeting, or a service request. This page is a maintained-source map for those questions.

Last checked: August 21, 2026. For emergencies, call 911. Agency phone numbers, office hours, eligibility, and service procedures can change; use the official page linked with each resource.

EMERGENCY & SAFETY

Immediate danger or medical emergency?

Call 911. Millbrae fire and emergency medical services are provided by Central County Fire Department, which also publishes community preparedness and fire-safety information.

REPORT A CITY ISSUE

Use Millbrae Works for non-emergency requests.

For potholes, streetlights, public-space problems, and other quality-of-life issues, the City uses Millbrae Works powered by SeeClickFix. Do not use it for an emergency.

Open Millbrae Works ↗

WATER & SEWER ACCOUNT

Separate service from utility billing.

Public Works maintains water and sewer service; Utility Billing handles starting, ending, and account questions. The City’s utility pages explain which office owns each step.

Open Utility Billing ↗

POLICE REPORT

File eligible non-emergency reports online.

The City’s online reporting system is for qualifying incidents within Millbrae with no known suspect and no State Freeway connection. Emergency or in-progress situations belong with 911.

Check online-report eligibility ↗

ALERTS

Subscribe before the disruption.

City News Flash, Public Works notifications, and countywide SMC Alert can provide updates about road work, water issues, storms, and other disruptions. Alerts are not a replacement for emergency dispatch.

Planning a project at an address?

Building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, demolition, planning, and home-occupation questions may use different City review paths. Start with the City’s permit guidance before beginning work.

FOOD, HOUSING & BASIC NEEDS

Need help staying housed or fed?

San Mateo County’s Core Service Agencies connect residents to food, emergency housing, utility assistance, crisis support, and referrals. Start there rather than guessing which program owns the need.

PUBLIC BENEFITS

Ask about food, cash, or health coverage.

The County Human Services Agency explains application pathways for CalFresh, CalWORKs, General Assistance, CAPI, and Medi-Cal. Eligibility and program rules change; use the maintained application source.

Review financial-assistance programs ↗

VOTING & ELECTIONS

Register, check status, or find a Vote Center.

San Mateo County’s Elections office maintains registration, ballot, Vote Center, drop-box, and election-information paths. Election dates and locations should always come from the current election office.
