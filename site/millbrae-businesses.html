BUSINESSES & COMMUNITY

Find local places with the labels intact.

A local business directory is useful when people can tell who supplied the information, what was independently selected, and whether a placement is paid. Millbrae Local keeps those paths separate.

Trust rule: Community listings are owner-maintained and should be verified with the business. Editorial guides are selected for a stated purpose and checked against official business information. Paid placements are labeled.

How to use a listing

A listing is a starting point, not a guarantee.

  1. Check the category and address.Make sure the business fits the need and is actually in the Millbrae service area.
  2. Open the direct website or call.Hours, menus, availability, prices, and services can change after a listing is published.
  3. Look for the label.Community-submitted, editorial, and paid placements should never be made to look identical.
