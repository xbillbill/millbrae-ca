HISTORY · NAME · LAND

What “Millbrae” remembers.

The name carries the landscape and the Mills estate, while the city’s longer history reaches through the 1835 Sánchez land grant, the railroad era, the 1906 depot, and incorporation on January 14, 1948. The date is cross-checked against San Mateo County LAFCO and a California appellate decision.

What makes a good local story

Specific enough to check. Human enough to remember.

  1. Begin with a real place or question.The station, a park, a school calendar, a street, a name, or a civic decision.
  2. Show the source trail.Readers should be able to inspect the official record, not just trust a polished paragraph.
  3. Add meaning without inventing certainty.Interpretation is welcome when it is labeled as interpretation and grounded in the documented facts.
