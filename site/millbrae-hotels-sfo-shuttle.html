Millbrae hotels with an SFO shuttle: two current options.

At the airport

Go up to Departures—even after arriving.

1

Reach the Departures/Ticketing Level. Follow signs or use the terminal elevator after collecting bags.

2

Walk to the center island. Use the signed Hotel Courtesy Shuttle Zone outside your terminal.

3

Confirm the hotel name. SFO lists kiosk dial codes 57 for Aloft and 30 for Westin.

4

Call overnight. Both hotels require an on-call request during their overnight windows.

Before booking

Common questions.

Which Millbrae hotels have a shuttle to SFO?

SFO's current hotel-shuttle directory lists Aloft Hotel and Westin Hotel with Millbrae as the city. Confirm the shuttle with the hotel when booking because airport and hotel operations can change.

Where do hotel shuttles pick up at SFO?

SFO directs travelers to the Hotel Courtesy Shuttle Zone on the center island of the Departures/Ticketing Level roadway at every terminal.

Do the shuttles run overnight?

Both hotels publish an overnight on-call window. Aloft lists 1:00–3:30 AM and Westin lists 1:05–3:35 AM. Call the hotel when ready for pickup.
