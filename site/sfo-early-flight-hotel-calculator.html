Before booking

Common questions.

How early should I arrive at SFO?

SFO says most airlines recommend arriving two hours before domestic departures and three hours before international departures. Check directly with your airline and replace the calculator default when its guidance differs.

Does a nearby hotel guarantee more sleep?

No. A slow or on-call transfer can erase the advantage. Use the actual pickup schedule, transfer time, walking time, and a missed-shuttle buffer.

What cost should I enter?

Enter only the added cost of the hotel plan: the room and changed transportation, minus parking, rideshare, or other costs you would have paid in the home plan.

Where can I compare nearby options?

Use the Millbrae hotel comparison, check the current shuttle guide, compare room-and-parking bundles in the park-and-fly calculator, or calculate usable room time during a connection with the layover hotel calculator.
