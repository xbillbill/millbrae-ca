CIVIC LIFE · EVENTS · NEWS

Know what is happening—and what is actually decided.

A useful local news desk starts with the calendar, the agenda, the announcement, and the named source. It then adds the context a resident or visitor needs to understand why the update matters.

Editorial standard: We distinguish an event from an announcement, a proposal from an adopted decision, and a source document from a summary. Dates and links stay visible so readers can check the original.

How a local update becomes useful

Follow the evidence trail.

QuestionBest first sourceWhat we add
When is it happening?Official calendarLocation, audience, and practical context
What was proposed?Agenda or staff reportPlain-language summary and next decision date
What was decided?Minutes, resolution, or adopted noticeWhat changed, who is affected, and what comes next
Is it developing?Named official or primary sourceTimeline, links, and a visible last-checked date

WHAT WE WON’T DO

No anonymous certainty.

We will not turn a rumor, a social post, or an undated screenshot into a “local fact.” If the source is unclear, the label stays clear.
